## Key ownership rules
- Organization owns its training-plan templates and organization training score.
- University owns academic framework, academic decisions and academic grade.
- SAQL owns workflow state, permissions, audit and platform governance—not the parties' business decisions.
- Student-submitted academic evidence keeps a trust state; it is not silently promoted to university-verified.
